The only roadblock is yourself.

After five intense years of running my successful million-dollar design agency, I hit a major roadblock. Burnout didn't just knock on my door—it barged right in and left me bedridden for three days straight. I didn't want to get up or face the world. Lying there, I thought to myself, "Okay, this is enough."

The next day, I made the tough decision to let go of all my employees. I knew I needed a break—a real one—to rethink my priorities and figure out what I truly wanted in life.

Growing up in France as a shy Asian kid in a mostly Western culture, I often felt like I didn't quite fit in. I was quiet and kept to myself. Design became my safe space—a way to express my creativity without having to say a word. But deep down, I was super ambitious, always juggling side projects and trying to "build something" meaningful.

That constant drive helped me achieve a lot, but it also led me straight into burnout.

As I lay there, I couldn't help but wonder: How can I bounce back? How can I make sure this never happens again? Those questions sparked an inward journey—a deep dive into understanding the myself and my true values. I realized that overcoming burnout wasn't just about resting; it was about transforming my mindset. And building a successful business requires a true understanding of yourself. I made the promise to help the next generation build strong foundations so they never go through the same stage as me.

If you are like me, you want to be a successful business without sacrificing your life. You want to unlock a new level of effectiveness and fulfillment. You want to feel motivated and excited every day. You want to feel confident with your decision towards your goals. You want to prioritize what matters, manage your emotions like a boss, and build habits that support your professional and personal growth. You want to meet and create deep connections with peers in the same stage as you.

Join me in this journey.

This is the reason I created Founder Foundry, a community of young and ambitious business owners that help each other towards our journey that we call entrepreneurship.
